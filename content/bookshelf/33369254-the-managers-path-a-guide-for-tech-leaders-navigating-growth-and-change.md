Read this because it seemed to be fairly universally listed on reading lists for engineering managers, especially for new managers. I felt like the examples were clear and relevant, and when a practice was recommended (for example, defaulting to weekly 1:1s) it was clearly explained why that practice versus alternatives ("so you can miss one; missed bi-weekly 1:1s must be rescheduled").<br/><br/>I am also amused to see that some classmates of mine read and rated this very same book seven years ago. The advice therein seems to have held up well despite all of the things that have happened in society since then.
