Aleksic makes some fairly astute observations, such as how creators “cross-post… and as such try to work within the most severe algorithmic constraints” and how middle-schoolers “aren’t saying ‘unalive’ to avoid algorithmic censorship, they do so because they see other people using it.” He then shows how human tendencies and algorithms form a cycle that cause new words to be formed and then escape into the offline world at an increasingly rapid pace, and notes that corporations use these microlabels to sell you more stuff.<br/><br/>I didn't realize y'all were following this guy on Instagram this whole time until after I'd read the book. Saw it on a bookshelf at the local bookstore.<br/><br/>Algospeak is a good and quick read, especially if you're a dev at a social network or just perpetually online.
