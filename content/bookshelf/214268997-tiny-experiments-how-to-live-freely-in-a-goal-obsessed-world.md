Compelling refutations of a lot of common self-help advice like "find your passion" or "focus on outcomes" that we viserally know are deeply unsatisfying and frustrating. I like the idea of The Pact -- "I will [action] for [duration]" -- and found it to be an especially compelling reframing. There's a clear awareness that people rarely succeed in adopting new habits or keeping new years resolutions, and so Le Cunff gives you new tools, processes, and ways to think about change to maximize the likelihood that you'll succeed in running your experiment. I feel like tiny experiments was the answer I have been looking for to everything I hated about Atomic Habits.
