Clear, easy to read, and well structured. Chapters are organized around specific behaviors. Each starts with a very short example of something a parent might say about a child's behavior, explains what might lead a child to act that way, and then provides clear concrete examples of things to say and do in response to that behavior.<br/><br/>Put this on my to-read list because I saw a LinkedIn post where someone said that reading it helped them to become a better manager.<br/><br/>There are a few paragraphs that talk about Internal Family Systems, which is a psychotherapy model that I don't agree with, but otherwise was an interesting read.
