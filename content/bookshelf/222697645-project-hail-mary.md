started reading this because the motion picture opens in theaters this weekend, and once i picked it up i couldn't put it down. coming from reading the bobiverse series there were a lot of familiar elements. i also love the way that the author uses the math to add a sense of urgency to the story -- i later saw a video where Weir shows a giant multi-page spreadsheet with all the calculations he did to work out the numbers. that said, the book is written in the first person from the perspective of the male protagonist and very heavy on the sci-fi elements so if those are deal-breakers for you maybe just watch the movie instead.
