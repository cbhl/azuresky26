really liked how clear and concise the advice in this book was. while it does include examples from the authors' lives, it does so in sideboxes that contrast different ways of approaching the same advice and avoids being rambly to the same extent other self-help books do. (there are actually additional strategies in the second half of the book!)<br/><br/>that said, not a panacea. small tweaks and a good reminder of a lot of advice i've heard before, but if you're truly overwhelmed and approaching burnout (as many are in this economy), this isn't going to fix that.<br/><br/>seems like a timely read given the popularity of Focus Friend by Hank Green this past month, too.<br/>
